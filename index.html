Writing

Essays on data quality, governance, and decision-making in the age of AI — the ideas underneath the apps.

Browse the writing →

We are a small studio by design. Small enough to answer to no one but the people who use what we make; deliberate enough to hold every product to a single standard — does this genuinely help someone decide, and can they trust it completely? If the answer to either half is no, it doesn't ship.

That standard is not a slogan. It comes from years spent building the kind of data systems that organisations bet real money on — where a number that couldn't be trusted was worse than no number at all. We build consumer software to that same bar, and see no reason a person should expect less rigour than a boardroom does.

What we're really doing is moving good judgement closer to you.

The intelligence that used to live inside large, private systems — the reasoning that turns messy inputs into a decision you can act on — no longer needs a data centre or a login. It can run on the device already in your hand. Cobalt InFX exists to put that capability where it belongs: with the individual, working privately, entirely under their own control.

What we build for

  • Reasoning that earns its answer, not features that fill a list
  • Everything processed on your own device, by default
  • Candour about uncertainty — estimates and unknowns shown as they are
  • Decisions handed to you, ready to act on
  • Precision and control, kept firmly in your hands

What we won't do

  • Collect, sell, or quietly upload your data
  • Treat your attention as something to capture
  • Dress up a guess as a confident answer
  • Lock the essentials behind a paywall
  • Track you, anywhere, for any reason

ChargeSort

Public charging, ranked by what a charge will actually cost you — and how long you will actually be there. Built for UK EV drivers.

🔌
Nine UK networks
🚗
Priced for your car

App Store link — to add at launch

iPhone · iOS 18 or later

💷

The energy price you will pay

Headline pence-per-kWh is rarely the whole story. ChargeSort works out the cost of the charge you need — your car, your connector, your current battery level — including connection and overstay fees where operators charge them.

⏱️

How long you will really be there

A 150 kW charger does not give every car 150 kW. Time is estimated from your car's own charging curve and the battery level you are starting from, so a cheap charger that takes an hour longer does not look like a bargain.

🗺️

Availability

See which bays are free, in use, or out of service — every reading carries the time it was last seen. We refresh status roughly every 12 minutes, so we show you the age of the data rather than calling it live.

🏠

Home is a charging option too

Enter your home tariff and ChargeSort compares public charging against charging at home — including the cheap overnight window on a time-of-use tariff, or a single flat rate if you use a communal or workplace charger.

💳

Is a membership worth it?

Subscriptions and memberships pay off at some mileages and not others. ChargeSort shows the break-even maths for how you actually charge, per operator, and leaves the decision to you.

🎯

It says when it does not know

Where an operator does not publish a rate, ChargeSort shows the price as unknown rather than guessing. Estimated figures are labelled as estimates. Nothing is dressed up as more certain than it is.

🧭

Plan a journey, not just a stop

Set a destination and ChargeSort works out where to charge along the way, matched to your car and the range you want to keep — free for everyone. With Plus it picks those stops for cost-effective charging across all networks, applying any memberships you hold.

📈

See what you spent — and saved

Charge at a recommendation, confirm it, and ChargeSort keeps a running history of your spend and the savings against the pricier options — all on your phone, never uploaded.

🔍

Filter the map to what you can use

Browse chargers on the map, filtered by operator and power and priced for your car. Zoom out and the counts still match your filters; on Plus you choose whether pins show the cheapest, fastest or balanced price.
